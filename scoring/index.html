Cribbage Scoring Guide

Every cribbage hand is scored with the starter card, so you count five cards. Here are all the ways to score.

Scoring in the show

CombinationPoints
Each different combination of cards totaling 152
Pair2
Three of a kind (pair royal)6
Four of a kind (double pair royal)12
Run of three or more consecutive ranks1 per card
Flush: 4 hand cards of one suit4
Flush: hand plus starter of one suit5
Crib flush (all 5 cards must match)5
Nobs: Jack of the starter's suit in hand1

Scoring during play

15 = 2, 31 = 2, pair = 2, three of a kind = 6, four of a kind = 12, runs = 1 per card, go or last card = 1. If the starter is a Jack, the dealer scores 2 for his heels.

Double runs

A pair inside a run doubles the run. For example 4-5-5-6 contains two runs of three (4-5-6 twice) plus a pair: 3 + 3 + 2 = 8 points before fifteens. Common names: double run (8), double double run like 4-4-5-5-6 (16 plus fifteens), and triple run like 4-5-5-5-6 (15 plus fifteens).

Worked example

Hand 5-5-10-J with a 5 starter, Jack matching the starter's suit:

  • Fifteens: each 5 with the 10 (3 ways), each 5 with the J (3 ways), and 5-5-5 = 7 fifteens = 14
  • Three of a kind = 6
  • Nobs = 1

Total: 21 points. Try your own hands in the cribbage hand calculator.

Impossible scores

A hand cannot score 19, 25, 26 or 27. The maximum is 29.
