Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information sluggerscribbageclub.org ("we") collects and how it is used.

Information stored in your browser

Your game statistics and an unfinished game are saved in your own browser using local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it with the "Reset stats" button or by clearing your browser data.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages are useful. These tools use cookies and collect information such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location. They do not tell us who you are.

Advertising

We may show ads from Google AdSense and other ad partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads or analytics cookies are used.

Children

This site is a general-audience card game site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete data held about you. Because we do not hold accounts or personal profiles, most data lives only in your browser or with the analytics and ad providers named above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@sluggerscribbageclub.org.
