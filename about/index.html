About Sluggers Cribbage Club

Sluggers Cribbage Club is a free cribbage site for players of every level. You can play a full game of two-player cribbage against the computer in your browser, with no download and no account.

What we offer

  • A free cribbage game with three difficulty levels, a real pegboard, hints and automatic scoring
  • A cribbage hand calculator that explains every point
  • Plain-English guides to the rules, scoring and strategy

Rules we follow

The game uses standard two-player rules: six cards each, two to the crib, play to 121, his heels for a Jack starter, and a crib flush only when all five cards match. Scoring is automatic, so muggins is not used.

Fair play

Cards are shuffled at random for every hand. The computer never sees your cards or the starter in advance; it plays only from its own hand.
