How to Play Cribbage

  1. Deal. Each player gets 6 cards. The dealer switches every hand.
  2. Crib. Both players put 2 cards face down into the crib. The crib belongs to the dealer.
  3. Starter. A card is cut from the deck and turned face up. If it is a Jack, the dealer scores 2 ("his heels").
  4. Play (pegging). Players take turns laying cards, adding to a running count that may not go over 31. Say "Go" when you can't play. Points are scored during play.
  5. Show. The non-dealer counts their hand first, then the dealer counts their hand and the crib, each together with the starter card.

Cribbage Strategy Tips

  • Keep 5s and cards that make 15s with them (10s, face cards).
  • When it's not your crib, avoid throwing 5s or pairs into it.
  • During pegging, avoid leading a card that lets your opponent make 15 easily, and avoid leaving the count at 5 or 21.

Cribbage FAQ

What is the highest possible cribbage hand?

29 points: three 5s plus the Jack in hand, with the fourth 5 of the Jack's suit as the starter card.

What score do you play to in cribbage?

A standard game is played to 121 points, which is twice around a standard pegboard.

Is a hand of 19 possible in cribbage?

No. 19 is impossible, as are 25, 26 and 27. That is why players jokingly call a zero-point hand a “19”.

Who counts first in cribbage?

The non-dealer always counts first, then the dealer counts their hand, then the dealer counts the crib.

Can you play cribbage online free?

Yes. This page lets you play cribbage free against the computer with no download or sign-up.

What does “his nobs” mean?

Holding the Jack of the same suit as the starter card scores 1 point, called his nobs.
