Best Cribbage Hands

The perfect 29 hand

Three 5s and a Jack in your hand, with the fourth 5 as the starter in the same suit as your Jack. It scores 16 for fifteens, 12 for four of a kind and 1 for nobs. It is very rare, roughly once in over 200,000 hands.

28 points

Four 5s with any 10-value card (or the same 29 hand without nobs): 16 for fifteens plus 12 for four of a kind.

24 points

Examples include 4-4-5-5 with a 6 starter, 3-3-3-3 with a 9 starter, and 7-7-8-8 with a 9 starter.

Impossible scores

19, 25, 26 and 27 cannot be scored with any hand. Players say "19" to mean a hand worth zero.
